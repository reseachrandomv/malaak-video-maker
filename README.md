# 🎬 Malaak Video Maker

Make short vertical (9:16) videos with voiceover, subtitles and music. Free.

## Option 1: Google Colab (phone friendly)
[![Open in Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/assets/colab-badge.svg)](https://colab.research.google.com/github/reseachrandomv/malaak-video-maker/blob/main/MalaakVideoMaker.ipynb)

1. Open the link and sign in with Google.
2. Tap ▶️ play. If asked, tap **Run anyway**.
3. Wait 3-5 minutes, then tap the big link.

## Option 2: GitHub Codespaces
[![Open in GitHub Codespaces](https://github.com/codespaces/badge.svg)](https://codespaces.new/reseachrandomv/malaak-video-maker)

1. Open https://codespaces.new/reseachrandomv/malaak-video-maker and tap **Create codespace**.
2. Wait for setup (about 5 minutes the first time).
3. Tap the **Ports** tab and open port **8501** (Malaak Video Maker).
4. When done, **stop the codespace** (github.com/codespaces → ⋯ → Stop) to save your free hours.

Optional keys: add `PEXELS_API_KEY` / `GEMINI_API_KEY` as Codespaces secrets, or type them in the Colab form. Never put keys in files.

⚠️ Download your videos before the session ends. They are deleted after.

No keys? Choose "my own script" and upload your own images or clips. For true stories, check the facts and name your source.
